THE AI ATTACK SURFACE

A conventional pentest looks for flaws in the server: SQL injection, broken auth, privilege escalation. An AI system adds a layer on top of that. An attacker doesn't always need to breach the backend — they can manipulate what the model does with the instructions and context it's given, so the model itself becomes the attack mechanism. That's the layer Prompt Shield watches.

About this engine

Prompt Shield's detection engine and Attack Lab simulations are powered by a Claude-based classifier and are provided for research and educational purposes. Scores are heuristic judgments from a language model, not a guarantee — they can miss novel attacks or misjudge unusual-but-benign text. Don't rely on this as your only safeguard for a production AI system.
